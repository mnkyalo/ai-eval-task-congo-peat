Core MBK-2 -- Mbeko interfluvial peatland
Cuvette Centrale, Likouala Department, Republic of the Congo

Latitude          +0.6800  (decimal degrees, WGS84)
Longitude         +18.3200
Elevation         318 m a.s.l.
Cored             2019-03-11
Core length       540 cm to mineral substrate; this dataset covers 0-140 cm
Setting           Interfluvial (rain-fed) peatland on the divide between two
                  blackwater tributaries. No riverine input; the site receives
                  water and dust only from the atmosphere.
Rainfall regime   Bimodal, ~1750 mm yr-1, driven by the twice-yearly passage of
                  the Intertropical Convergence Zone.
Vegetation today  Mixed swamp forest; Raphia laurentii locally dominant with
                  Uapaca and Alchornea; herbaceous openings on the margins.
Sampling          Pollen at 2 cm intervals (odd cm). AMS 14C on bulk peat at
                  10 cm intervals.
Pollen counting   Sub-samples were spiked with Lycopodium tablets before
                  preparation; the preparation record for each level is in
                  spike_and_volume.csv. Counted to a minimum sum of 1000
                  grains, terrestrial and herbaceous pollen only. Spores
                  excluded from the sum.
